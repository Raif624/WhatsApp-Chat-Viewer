WhatsApp Chat Viewer
About the project

Read and explore your exported WhatsApp chats

WhatsApp Chat Viewer is a web-based archive viewer that helps you read and organize WhatsApp conversations you have exported from your own account. Import an export ZIP, browse messages in a chat-style interface, search conversations, and open supported attachments included in the archive.

Quick user guide

How to use WhatsApp Chat Viewer

Follow these steps to export a conversation from WhatsApp and view it in your archive.

Export a chat from WhatsApp

On your phone, open the individual or group conversation you want to archive. Open the chat menu, choose Export chat, and select whether to include media.

Save the exported file

Save or transfer the exported ZIP file to the device you will use to access the Chat Viewer. Including media can make the export file larger.

Sign in to your account

Open WhatsApp Chat Viewer, create an account or sign in, and go to the chat viewer dashboard.

Import your chat ZIP

Choose the import option, select the WhatsApp exported ZIP file, review the detected chat in the preview, and confirm the import.

Read and explore messages

Open the imported conversation from your dashboard. Read the messages in the chat-style view and move between your imported conversations.

Search and open attachments

Use text search, sender filters, and date filters to find messages. Open or download supported media and documents that were included in the export.

What you can do

Features

Browse conversations

Read exported messages in a chat-style layout and move between imported conversations.

Search and filter

Find messages using text search, sender filters, and date filters.

View supported attachments

Open or download supported images, videos, audio files, and documents included in your export.

Access your own archive

Sign in to access the chats associated with your account. Your imported archive can be accessed from another device after signing in.

Privacy and responsible use: Imported conversations are associated with the account used to import them. Account-based access controls are intended to keep each user's archives separate. Only import conversations and media that you are authorised to access. Avoid using a shared or public device to access private archives.
